About This Map

Welcome to Northeastern Illinois University!

Whether you're a new student trying to find your way around campus or just curious about what NEIU has to offer, come take a stroll with me! I've highlighted several places around campus that I've found helpful, from places to study and grab a coffee to spaces for art, recreation, and connecting with other students. This campus has so much to offer.

Use the Let's Explore! menu to choose a destination, or click on one of the little Goldie markers to discover what's inside each building. You can also switch between street and satellite views using the map controls.

I hope this map helps you find your way around and discover a few places you might not have known about!
